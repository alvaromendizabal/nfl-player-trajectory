Inspect the method and the limits

Three deliberately small references

Constant velocity extends the last observed frame-to-frame displacement. Hold position repeats the last coordinates. Damped velocity multiplies displacement by the damping factor at each future step. No parameters are fitted.

Known synthetic provenance

Six invented games contain two plays and three tracks each, sampled at 10 Hz. The 8-frame observed window is separate from 12 future frames. This interface evaluates the four plays in the two preassigned held-out games.

A demonstration, not a benchmark

These are not real player trajectories. Changing controls repeatedly explores one small fixture; it does not establish generalization or evaluate the project’s private neural ensemble. No field clipping or player interaction model is applied.
