## Important unanswered questions

The diagnostic has used limited training plays, one seed and repeatedly reused validation games. Those limitations differ from the winning system's representation, augmentation, training exposure and ensemble. Their individual contribution to the performance gap is unknown. It is not justified to declare the entire gap a feature problem, nor to treat a rejected narrow interface as proof that a domain signal is useless.

High-value avenues still include stronger temporal/player representations, properly isolated role/arrival behavior, trajectory target parameterization, domain-preserving augmentation, training-data coverage, feature-family omissions and independent chronological evaluation. Earlier negative augmentation or geometric probes should not be silently recycled without a changed mechanism and documented rationale. No new external dataset is integrated in this milestone.

Employer-facing consolidation of manually executed notebooks and evidence into canonical repository artifacts remains outstanding. These downloaded kits are NOT merged into GitHub merely because they ran in the AWS space.
