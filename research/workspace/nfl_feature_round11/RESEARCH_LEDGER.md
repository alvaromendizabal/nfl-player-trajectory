# Research ledger — feature research remains open

## Evidence carried forward

Round 1 arrival helped the small ridge diagnostic; it did not transfer to the later tree comparison. Earlier-origin mixing, turning/braking additions, direct-state later-fold replication, passing-line features, and historical-response treatments did not earn retention in their tested interfaces. Round 8 history and terminal encoders were almost indistinguishable; Round 9 confirmed real input differences but limited prediction sensitivity. Round 10 goal values improved the matched mean score by 1.51%, yet failed uncertainty and preserved-tree gates.

These are conditional results, not proof that entire feature families are universally useless. Several repeated small-sample studies do not demonstrate exhaustive domain investigation or a competitive end-to-end model.

Training-only coverage inventory; final-checkpoint training error without refitting; six direct receiver-relative channels; six adjacent-frame motion channels; feature support/tail evidence. New channels are not yet integrated, screened for accuracy, ablated or temporally replicated. No predictive acceptance claim is allowed.

## Highest-value next choices, conditional on this audit

- Training coverage: quantify unused plays within the same fixed training-game set before choosing a justified scale-only experiment. No new split or sample is selected in this package.
- Information delivery: receiver-relative sequences are explicit in a leading solution; compare them with a matched masked channel control only after support and capacity are assessed.
- Learning dynamics: final training error and recorded learning curves must inform a diagnosis; never infer capacity or overfitting solely from one scalar difference.
- Broader modeling: auxiliary motion supervision, trajectory parameterization, longer training support and observation-origin augmentation remain possible directions. Their earlier experiments and artifact lineage must be reconciled before reuse. They are not executed here.
- Validation and integration: stop repeated tuning on the same 14 evaluation games. Define a larger, reproducible and appropriately isolated protocol before claiming confirmation. Recover/use the canonical full forecasting pipeline instead of treating a diagnostic model as the final submission.
- External seasons: competition permission, event alignment, role derivation and inference availability must be explicitly established; nothing external is downloaded here.
