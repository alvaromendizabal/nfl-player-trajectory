Do not claim algorithms account for none of the remaining gap: feature representation, model interface, loss, data, validation and ensembling have not been quantitatively separated. The immediate priority remains feature quality, not algorithm sweeps. These small diagnostics must lead to a real model integration decision, not endless cheap studies.

Primary research: [step-and-turn movement](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17866v1), [circular directional modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06122), [temporal/player coverage relationships](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.25901v1). These motivate mechanisms, not guaranteed gains on the Prediction competition.

Benchmark **0.46340** remains the research target. No report in this package establishes a new Kaggle score, rank, feature-space maturity, or a 9.9/10 release rating.
