# Feature research ledger · evidence before expansion

## Current evidence

The small ridge studies supported role-conditioned arrival; the subsequent fixed-tree/sample experiment did not. Turning, braking and orientation-response additions and the tested earlier-origin mixture did not earn scaling. The fixed-tree terminal/history relationship representations also failed. These negatives apply to the tested interfaces and populations, not every possible learned trajectory representation.

Round 4 direct state helped a reused first fold by 2.2743%; goal geometry did not add value. That passing family must now be replicated before being treated as an established improvement.

## Active hypothesis — Round 5

Expose the 62 observed-state fields separately from forecast-time response terms. Keep the representation and estimator frozen and test later chronological windows. This is feature engineering through contribution and stability testing, not another algorithm phase. New candidate definitions: zero. New fits: at most eight. First-fold fits: zero. No automatic feature promotion.

## Conditional next investigations (not launched)

1. **Direct-state attribution and integration.** If later-fold evidence survives, ablate instantaneous player/role/support state separately from multiwindow motion/receiver context under equal row/model conditions, then integrate the justified representation into a replayable forecasting path. A direct-state bundle gain does not prove all 62 fields contribute individually. Freeze a distinct comparison before fitting; avoid repeatedly selecting variants on the same games.

2. **Learned observed temporal relationships.** The official winning solution retains short observed histories and mixes player information. Revisit only with a materially different, parameter-matched temporal interface and correctly synchronized observations; do not relaunch the failed fixed pair-summary treatment unchanged. Keep neighbor geometry distinct from actual defender-responsibility annotations, which are not available as labels here.

3. **Trajectory representation and historical context.** Test stable displacement/velocity parameterizations, role-conditioned latent trajectory concepts and truly earlier-game context only with frozen controls, training-only fitted encoders and inference-availability tests. Do not conflate extra training or a changed loss with feature value. Do not add another season without verifying official access, schema, task timing and competition rules. Do not resurrect the earlier-origin mixture by tuning its weight against its failed fold.

## Model gap versus feature gap

The winning author's system also used architectural, augmentation, loss and ensemble choices. Available evidence does not allow assigning the 0.46340 gap wholly to features or wholly to algorithms. This workflow deliberately keeps the estimator fixed to answer feature questions. It does not claim that a compact histogram-tree screen exhausts what neural/learned representations can exploit.

## Stop criteria

No new broad feature bank is launched simply because there are many plausible football concepts. Each proposed family needs input availability, mechanism, matched baseline, leakage tests, train-only screening, bounded fits, ablation, cross-time evidence and exact replay. Major plausible high-value avenues remain open. “Research-grade” is an evidence standard, not a synonym for column count or a promised leaderboard position.
