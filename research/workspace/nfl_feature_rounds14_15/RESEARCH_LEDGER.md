The explicit representation questions are conditional on the same complete
Round13motion inputs. This does not reverse the old scientific gate. Their 24
new channels are transformations of existing observed information, not 24newraw
signals. Numerical usefulness and real-time availability must be demonstrated.

After readiness, prioritize a same-model data-scale-only comparison when the
unused labels are sound and the budget is feasible. Do not scale data and add
features in the same attribution arm. A reviewed decision may release the two
prepared exploratory studies, defer them, or request a targeted data correction.
Feature-first does not imply features-only. We cannot assign percentages of the
leaderboard gap to causes from current evidence.

External/historical-season augmentation, sequence-loss choices, role-conditioned
behavior, symmetry-respecting augmentation and complementary ensembles remain
separate questions requiring rule checks and controlled evaluation. Previously
negative tests constrain their tested interface, not every conceivable encoder.
Avoid another indefinite sequence of small proxy screens without stronger
training coverage and inference/validation integration.
