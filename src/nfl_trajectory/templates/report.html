Evaluation contract

Official coordinate RMSE = √[Σ(dx² + dy²)/(2N)]. Lower is better. ADE weights player-frame positions; FDE weights complete player trajectories. Confidence intervals resample whole games 2,000 times. The fixed validation set supports model development; its scores are not an unbiased final estimate after repeated selection. The 48-game holdout remains unscored.

The supplied landing coordinates and horizon are permitted competition inputs. A live forecast without those inputs is a different problem. These are local temporal-validation results, not Kaggle leaderboard scores or medal claims.
